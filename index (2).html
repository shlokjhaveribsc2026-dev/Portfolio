Momentum Investing in NIFTY 50 Stocks

Quantitative research project, 2026

I am studying whether a stock's past 6-month return relates to its next 3-month return. The dataset covers NIFTY 50 stocks and the NIFTY 50 Index (the benchmark) from August 2024 to August 2026, at 3-month intervals.

So far I have organised the raw price data in Excel, grouped by stock, with a data summary sheet, and written the research proposal in Word. The tests, correlation and simple linear regression, come next.
